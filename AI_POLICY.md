# Generative AI / LLM Policy

I appreciate that we can't realistically police how you author your contributions, which includes whether you employ large-language model (LLM)-based development tools. So, we don't.

However, due to both legal and human reasons, we have to establish boundaries. We take the responsibility for these projects very seriously and we expect you to take your responsibility for your contributions seriously, too. This used to be a given, but it changed now that a contribution is just one prompt away.

- Every contribution must be backed by a human who unequivocally owns the copyright for all changes.
  - No LLM bots in `Co-authored-by:`s or mentioned in commit messages.
- DoS-by-slop, whether by contribution or comments, leads to a permanent ban.
- Absolutely **no** unsupervised agentic tools.
- Do **not** trigger bots like Copilot review on this repository.
- Issues marked "good first issue" are reserved for new contributors. Automated or assisted contributions or alike will be closed without comment.

By submitting a pull request, you certify that:

- You are the author of the contribution or have the legal right to submit it.
- You either hold the copyright to the changes or have explicit legal authorization to contribute them under this project's license.
- You understand the code and are happy with its quality.
- You accept responsibility for it.

If you insist on spending your money in an attempt to contribute to the project, donate it directly instead. Don't spend it just to make slop whilst boiling the ocean.

For further details and justification, see the [`attrs` project's policy](https://github.com/python-attrs/attrs/blob/main/.github/AI_POLICY.md), on which this policy was based.

---

This policy governs the contributions to any and all projects which reference it, not just the repository which contains it.
